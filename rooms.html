The rooms

Eight rooms, and three kinds of morning.

Every room keeps its original floor and its own shape — no two are quite the same size. All have a private bathroom, cotton sheets, a kettle with village tea, heating in winter, and no television. Rates are per night for two people and include breakfast.

Garden Rooms

$95 a night

The three oldest rooms in the house sit level with the lower garden, each with its own door onto it. They were the winter rooms — thick walls, small deep windows, cool in August and easy to heat in January.

Best for a couple who want to come and go without crossing the hall, and for anyone who would rather not take the stone stair.

Arch Rooms

$135 a night

On the first floor, where the family used to receive. Each of the three has the triple-arched window that Douma is known for: three round-headed lights, the middle one taller, filling the room with light from about seven in the morning.

Qamar looks south down the valley, Nada east to the ridge, Warde over the roofs towards the souk. They are reached by the old stone stair — eighteen steps, no lift.

  • Writing table in the window, two armchairs
  • Sleeps two, or three with an extra bed at $25

Two suites

$175–$185 a night

The Liwan, at $185, is the old central hall: a vaulted ceiling, the widest arch in the house, a sitting end with a low table, and room for a fourth bed. It is the one families take.

The Roof Room, at $175, is up under the tiles with its own terrace — thirty square metres of it, facing west. In August people carry their mattress out there and nobody minds.

  • Sitting area, and a private terrace in the Roof Room
  • Sleeps three to four

Included

Breakfast from 8:00 to 10:30, Wi-Fi that reaches every room, parking inside the gate, heating from November to April, and tea and coffee in the hall at any hour.

Extras

Dinner on Friday and Saturday, four courses, $28 a head — tell us the day before. Extra bed $25. Airport transfer $70 each way. Laundry by the load, $10.

Minimum stay

Two nights on Fridays, Saturdays and public holidays. One night is fine from Sunday to Thursday.

Cancellation

Free up to seven days before you arrive. Inside seven days we charge one night. No deposit is taken to hold a room — we ask you to tell us if plans change.

Paying

Cash in US dollars or Lebanese pounds at the day's rate, on arrival. Bank transfer for groups taking the whole house. No cards — the village has no reliable line for them.

Children, dogs, stairs

Children are welcome; under-fives stay free in their parents' room. Dogs are fine in the garden but not in the rooms. The Garden Rooms are step-free from the parking terrace; the first floor is reached by eighteen stone steps and there is no lift.
